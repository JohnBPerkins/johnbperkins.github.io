What I actually do

      
    


    

      

        

          My background is backend and infrastructure — Go, gRPC, WebSockets, horizontally scalable
          services doing thousands of requests per second, containerized and shipped on AWS with
          Terraform and CI/CD. For the last two years that foundation has gone into AI systems:
          MCP servers, multi-agent orchestration, GraphRAG retrieval, and the boring, load-bearing
          plumbing that keeps them honest in production.
        

        

          What I care about is getting AI out of the demo and into production — grounded retrieval,
          graceful failure, and infrastructure that doesn't fold the first time real traffic shows up.
