About

My background is backend and infrastructure. I've built distributed systems in Go, gRPC and WebSockets, running thousands of requests per second on AWS. For the last two years that foundation has gone into AI systems: MCP servers, multi-agent orchestration, GraphRAG retrieval, and the load-bearing plumbing that keeps them honest in production.

  1. 01

  3. 03
